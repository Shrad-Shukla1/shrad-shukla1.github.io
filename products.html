Operational Research

Subseasonal to Seasonal
Forecast Products

Operational early warning systems developed through research at the Climate Hazards Center, UC Santa Barbara — supporting food security, drought preparedness, and agroclimatic monitoring across vulnerable regions worldwide.

The products below represent direct research contributions to operational forecast systems used by FEWS NET, NASA, USGS, and SERVIR partners. Each product is actively maintained and updated in near-real time. Click View Live Product on any card to access the latest maps and data.

Subseasonal Forecasts

Latest 15-day Precip Forecast
Updated Daily
CHIRPS-GEFS Precipitation Forecasts
Global 50°S–50°N 1–15 day lead Daily updates
Bias-corrected, high-resolution (0.05°) precipitation forecasts that seamlessly extend CHIRPS observed rainfall into 15-day outlooks. Used globally for agroclimatic and hydrologic hazards early warning, and featured in FEWS NET, GEOGLAM, and SERVIR ClimateSERV platforms.

30-Day Rainfall & Dry Spell Forecast
Updated Weekly
SubX West Africa Subseasonal Forecasts
West Africa 10–30 day lead Weekly updates NASA SERVIR
High-resolution (5 km), bias-corrected multi-model ensemble forecasts for West Africa, derived from the NMME SubX project. Maps show consecutive dry days, total rainfall, and anomalies — key metrics for agropastoral early warning in the Sahel and CILSS countries.

Regional rainfall maps combining the latest CHIRPS observations with CHIRPS-GEFS 15-day forecasts. Seasonal Rainfall Monitor and Recent Rainfall Monitor provide multi-period accumulations used to characterize evolving drought conditions and anticipate crop and pasture impacts.

Seasonal Forecasts

1 – 8 months ahead
🌍
Seasonal Precip & Temp Probability Maps
Updated Monthly (~7th)
CHC Seasonal MME Forecasts (NMME + ECMWF)
Global & Regional 1–8 month lead Monthly (7th) FEWS NET / NASA
Probabilistic tercile-category precipitation and temperature forecast maps derived from a 130+ member multi-model ensemble (NMME + ECMWF SEAS5). Validated against hydrometeorological events in East Africa and operationalized to support FEWS NET food security outlooks across Sub-Saharan Africa, Latin America, and Afghanistan.
View Live Product Shukla et al. 2019
☀️
Evaporative Demand Drought Outlooks
Experimental / Archived
EDDI Seasonal Forecast Product
Global 1–9 month lead CHC / NOAA PRISM
Forecasts of the Evaporative Demand Drought Index (EDDI) derived from CFSv2 model output, including temperature, wind speed, humidity, and radiation. Validation studies (Shukla et al. 2017) demonstrated that reference evapotranspiration forecasts outperform precipitation alone in major agricultural regions.

S2S Hydrological Forecasts

Soil moisture · Streamflow · Rootzone water deficit
💧
Rootzone Soil Moisture Forecast
Updated Monthly
NHyFAS / FLDAS-Forecast (Southern Africa)
Southern Africa 1–6 month lead Monthly NASA GSFC / FEWS NET
NASA's S2S hydrological forecast system combining FLDAS land surface models with NMME climate forecasts to generate ensemble rootzone soil moisture and streamflow predictions. Demonstrated ability to identify severe drought events (e.g., 2015–16 El Niño drought) at lead times of several months before harvest, enabling timely food insecurity early warning.

East Africa Special Reports
Ethiopia · Kenya · Somalia Season-to-date + outlook CHC / EMI / KMD / FAO SWALIM
Dekadally-updated seasonal reports blending CHIRPS data with national rain gauge networks (Ethiopia NMA, Kenya Meteorological Department, Somalia Meteorology), CHIRPS-GEFS outlooks, and analog-year methods. Directly informs FEWS NET food security outlooks for the Horn of Africa.

Afghanistan Agroclimatic Outlook
Monthly
Afghanistan Agroclimatic Outlook
Afghanistan Seasonal outlook CHC / FEWS NET / USGS
Monthly multi-variable report synthesizing precipitation, snowpack, streamflow, vegetation conditions, and seasonal climate forecasts across Afghanistan — a highly food-insecure country with a complex mountain hydrology. Research on ENSO-based drought outlooks for Afghanistan (Shukla et al. 2024) directly supports this product.

Monthly Crop Condition Bulletin
Monthly
GEOGLAM Crop Monitor for Early Warning
Sub-Saharan Africa, Latin America In-season + seasonal outlook GEOGLAM / CHC / NASA Harvest / FEWS NET
Monthly regional crop condition outlooks incorporating CHIRPS-GEFS rainfall forecasts, NMME seasonal forecasts, NDVI-based vegetation monitoring, and hydrological model output. CHC contributes climate and forecast assessments for food-insecure regions, with past outlooks having provided advance warning of major drought events.

Crop Yield Forecasts

Preseason · In-season · FEWS NET Countries
🌽
Preseason Maize & Wheat Yield Forecasts
Annual / Preseason
Preseason Crop Yield Forecasts (FEWS NET Countries)
Sub-Saharan Africa, S. Asia Preseason (months ahead) USGS / CHC / NASA Harvest
Statistical preseason forecasting system for maize and wheat yields using S2S climate forecasts, ENSO teleconnections, and Earth observation inputs. Provides actionable lead times for food crisis early warning before planting decisions are made. Published in Nature Communications (Anderson, Shukla et al. 2024) and operationalized through USGS-funded FEWS NET projects.
Anderson, Shukla et al. 2024 FEWS NET Portal
📊
ML-Based In-Season Crop Outlooks
In-season / Annual
Sub-Saharan Africa Maize Yield Forecasting System
Sub-Saharan Africa In-season to preseason CHC / USGS
Machine learning-based crop forecasting system combining Earth observation data (CHIRPS, MODIS NDVI, FLDAS soil moisture) with climate forecasts for subnational maize yield prediction across food-insecure regions in Sub-Saharan Africa. Research spanning panel, time-series, and spatial out-of-sample models supports continued operational development.
